Lines — Privacy Policy

Applies to the Lines app (web and iOS). Last updated: August 2026.

Lines is a task tracker. The only reason it stores anything is so your lines and tasks follow you between devices. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

What is stored

  • Your email address and a password — used solely to sign you in. The password is stored only in hashed form. If you sign in with Apple, Apple provides an email address (which may be a private relay address) instead of a password.
  • The content you create — workstreams, tasks, notes, dependencies, links between tasks, and inbox captures. This is your data; the app just keeps it.

That's the full list. Lines does not collect your location, contacts, photos, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or anything else.

Where it lives

Data is stored in a Supabase project (Postgres hosted on cloud infrastructure), protected by row-level security so an account can only ever read and write its own rows. Traffic between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS. The app also keeps a copy of your own data on your device so it works offline; that copy never leaves the device and is removed when you sign out.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications, planned on your device from your own tasks. No push server is involved and nothing about your notifications is transmitted anywhere.

Your data is yours

  • Export — Settings offers a full JSON export and a CSV export at any time.
  • Delete — Settings → Account → Delete my account permanently removes your account and everything in it from the server, immediately. There is no retention period and no copies are kept.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: open an issue on GitHub.
